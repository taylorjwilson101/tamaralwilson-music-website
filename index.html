About

Live life in song

Tamara Lyn Wilson is a singer/songwriter from North Carolina. She believes a great song should lift your heart, warm the soul and make you feel good.

Her party started when she was only a girl, singing gospel and country with her ‘Papa’ and her Dad in their down-home sing-alongs. Her journey has always led her back to the melodies of her heart and soul.
